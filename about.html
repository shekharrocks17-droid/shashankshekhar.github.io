MY STORY

I write what the heart remembers.

Shashank Shekhar is an author with a love for stories, creativity and the emotions hidden inside everyday moments.

With an MBA from NMIMS and experience in the corporate world, his professional journey exists alongside a deeply creative side drawn to writing, arts and crafts.

NarrativesByShashank is his space to share stories that explore love, relationships, choices, hope and the beautiful complexity of being human.

“A good story doesn't just tell you what happened. It makes you feel that you were there.”
